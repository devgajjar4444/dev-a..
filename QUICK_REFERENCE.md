# Quick Reference Guide - Adi & Dev's Love Arcade

## 🎮 All 6 Games at a Glance

### 1. Matrix for Adi 🎮
**In One Sentence**: Tic Tac Toe battle between lovers.  
**How to Play**: Click grid cells to place your mark (X or O).  
**How Many Can Play**: 2 (Adi vs Dev AI)  
**Duration**: 3-5 minutes  
**Best For**: Quick strategic challenges  
**Click = Place mark → Turn switches → Check for 3 in a row**

---

### 2. Love Lock 🔐
**In One Sentence**: Memory matching game with 5 emoji pairs.  
**How to Play**: Click cards to flip and find matching pairs.  
**How Many Can Play**: 2 (alternating turns)  
**Duration**: 2-4 minutes  
**Best For**: Shared nostalgia  
**Click = Flip card → Show emoji → Check match → Score point**

---

### 3. Cupid Shot 🏹
**In One Sentence**: Click falling hearts before they vanish.  
**How to Play**: Tap hearts as they fall from top to bottom.  
**How Many Can Play**: 1 (personal challenge)  
**Duration**: 1-2 minutes  
**Best For**: Reflex training, adrenaline rush  
**Click = Heart disappears → Score +10 → Next heart falls**

---

### 4. Quick Fingers ⚡
**In One Sentence**: Tap tiles as fast as possible.  
**How to Play**: Click tiles in rapid succession to beat your speed record.  
**How Many Can Play**: 1 (personal best chasing)  
**Duration**: 2-3 minutes  
**Best For**: Speed challenges  
**Click = Tile flashes → Combo builds → Speed increases**

---

### 5. Do You Love Dev? 💕
**In One Sentence**: Love calculator quiz that confirms Dev is 100% loved.  
**How to Play**: Answer yes/no questions about loving Dev.  
**How Many Can Play**: 2 (can do together)  
**Duration**: 2-3 minutes  
**Best For**: Lighthearted affirmation  
**Click = Answer question → Next Q appears → See final %**

---

### 6. Guess the Date 💌 ✨ (NEW!)
**In One Sentence**: Reveal 9 special dates by flipping cards like love letters.  
**How to Play**: Click cards to reveal romantic dates and memories.  
**How Many Can Play**: 1 (individual nostalgia trip)  
**Duration**: 3-5 minutes  
**Best For**: Celebrating relationship milestones  
**Click = Card flips → Date appears → Memory text shows → Progress updates**
